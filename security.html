Introduction to Web Security

Web security is all about protecting your personal data and ensuring safe browsing while you're online. Whether you're shopping, socializing, or learning, it's important to understand the risks and how to stay secure. At The Joy Lab, we care not just about your creativity but also about your online safety.

Why It Matters

Every time you visit a website or fill out a form, your data can be exposed if the site isn't secure. Knowing the basics helps you avoid scams and protect your identity.

Common Web Security Risks:

  • Data Exposure: Hackers can access personal info through insecure websites or storage.
  • Phishing: Fake emails or websites trick you into giving away sensitive information.
  • Insecure Websites: Websites using http:// instead of https:// can leak data.
  • Unsafe Extensions: Malicious browser extensions can steal your information or track you.
